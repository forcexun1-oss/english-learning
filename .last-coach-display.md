📝 Natural English: "What are you talking about? I'll download it and copy it over. If he knows how to use it, why would I be asking you?"
💡 Tip: In Chinese, rhetorical questions like '他还问你?' use '还' to mean 'still' or 'would...still'. In English, we add 'why would I be' to express the same implied frustration.
