📝 Natural English: "The credentials are fine — go ahead and test it."
💡 Tip: The particle 啊 just softens the tone and is normally dropped in English; "你测试一下" works best as a plain imperative ("go ahead and test it") rather than "you test it", which sounds like a contrast or accusation.
