📝 Natural English: "Just give me a list, and I'll have him copy it."
💡 Tip: The sentence-final particle here should be 吧 (suggestion), not 把 — 把 is a preposition meaning 'take/handle'. Also, '让他拷贝' is more naturally 'have him copy it' than 'let him copy it' when you're instructing someone.
