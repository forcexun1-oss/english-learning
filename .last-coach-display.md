📝 Natural English: "I'd like to discuss this."
💡 Tip: "Discuss" is transitive — say "discuss this / discuss the design", never "discuss about it" (unlike 关于...讨论). Also, "I'd like to" sounds slightly more polite than "I want to" in a work chat.
