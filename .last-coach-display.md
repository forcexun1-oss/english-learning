📝 Natural English: "I'm asking you: why is this happening?"
💡 Tip: In Chinese, '老子' is a colloquial and somewhat rude way to refer to yourself. In English, simply using 'I' is appropriate; adding 'the hell' can convey a similar tone, but it's better to keep it polite in a professional setting.
