**What was built**
- **Core** (`Core/Speech.cs`, no scene code):
  - a sentence splitter for streamed text;
  - a parser that pulls the growing `"line"` text out of the JSON reply as chunks arrive;
  - a priority queue: boss &gt; companion &gt; player &gt; minion. Higher interrupts lower, minion/player shouts and boss move callouts are dropped if anyone is talking, there are per-speaker cooldowns, and the queue is capped at 8;
  - pitch/speed tweaks per emotion, and the boss/minion/player line tables.
- **Streaming:** `PiRpcChannel.AskStreaming` passes each text chunk on as it arrives; `Ask` still behaves exactly as before. `CompanionBrain.SayStreaming` feeds it, and `AgentCoordinator` streams the greeting and the end-of-run reflection, filling the companion's speech bubble progressively. If no text arrives within 2.5 s (cold start, or the model busy with the boss plan), the rule-based line is spoken first. Fallbacks are spoken whole.
- **Game** (`Game/Speech/`):
  - `SpeechDirector`, added in `GameApp` with one line;
  - three engines: the Kokoro daemon, macOS `say`, and silent;
  - one 2D AudioSource for voice;
  - floating captions above the boss, minions and player (the companion keeps its bubble);
  - canned lines and the next boss taunt are synthesized ahead of time while idle.
- **Daemon:** `unity/Assets/StreamingAssets/Tts/kokoro_daemon.py`, adapted from mac-assistant with the same line-by-line JSON protocol. It adds request ids, speed, voice blends and a ready message.
- **No edits** to Actors, World, Bosses, Flow or Ui. Lines come from game events and from text already on screen (the companion bubble and the boss banner).
