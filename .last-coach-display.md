prompt: 不确定就去查一下,人家怎么干的,然后试一下,怎么可能不能并行嘛,那要等到什么时候,建好worktree不就行了
session: 681b5bfa-f037-4e84-a8e3-84b3ca10a8c1
---
📝 Natural English: "If you're not sure, go look it up — see how others do it, then try it. How could it possibly not be parallel? How long would we have to wait otherwise? Just create the worktree and you're set."
💡 Tip: 人家 here means "other people / others," not "he/she" — use "how others do it" rather than a pronoun. The rhetorical 怎么可能 works well as "how could it possibly...".

session: 681b5bfa-f037-4e84-a8e3-84b3ca10a8c1
---
📝 Natural English: "The talent system will be redesigned later anyway, right? Why can they still be linked horizontally right now? If the talents change, we just update the config, right? No need to touch the UI, right?"
