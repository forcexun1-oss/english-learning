📝 Natural English: "I've implemented part of it, let's take a look."
💡 Tip: In Chinese, '有' + verb (有实现) emphasizes the action, but in English, we use the present perfect tense directly without '有'. Also, '看看' is often translated as 'let's take a look' or 'check it out'.
