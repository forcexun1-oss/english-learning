**Major**

- **M1. The parry fix doesn't follow from the doc's own model** (design-method.md:292). The earliest reactive press is CP + r = hit − 0.20; the latest is hit − startup. Reorder the calibration list to match.
- **M2. The feint timeline is wrong.** The real swing takes 0.6 s. The cancel time is random within [0.15, CP − 0.10]. And a feint isn't guaranteed: CancelPolicy caps it by MaxCancelShare 0.25, Focus, and "no two in a row". Cancel-to-hit is 0.25 + 0.6 = 0.85 s, longer than the 0.8 s dash cooldown, so **"dash × feint ✕" flips** (T3:56) and "early parry ✕" gets weaker. *Fix:* redo T2/T3 from CancelPolicy and add feint rate as a measured input.
