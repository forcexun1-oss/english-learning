📝 Natural English: "Continue with the ones that haven't finished running yet."
💡 Tip: Chinese often drops the noun after 的 (还没跑完的 = 'the ones not finished yet'); in English you need a placeholder like 'the ones' or 'those'. Also, 吧 is just a softening particle and has no direct English equivalent.
