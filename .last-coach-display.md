## What it does
- **The tree:** 19 talents in four branches. 战技 (combat), 生存 (survival) and 机动 (mobility) act on the player; 伙伴 (companion) acts on the companion, except 同心, which adds player max HP.
- **Rules:**
  - Each talent can have prerequisites.
  - A tier opens only after `tier × 2` points are spent lower in the same branch.
  - Each player branch has one "pick one of two" pair at tier 3.
  - Talents have 1 to 3 ranks; capstones cost 2 points.
  - A full branch is 8 points, about the size of one boon, so talents don't outgrow the permanent upgrades.
- **Capstones** run as event hooks during a run:
  - 闪击: next attack +50% within 2.5 s after a dash.
  - 残影: +30% move speed for 1.5 s after a dash.
  - 背水: +25% attack and +10% speed below 35% HP.
  - 守护之光: when a hit leaves you below 30% HP, the companion heals 20 × its skill power, once per room.
  - Every other talent is a plain stat bonus written as `talent:&lt;id&gt;`.
- **Points:** for now, 1 to start, +1 per boss kill (old saves' wins count), +1 per 3 rooms cleared. I didn't use the new player levels from main because they reset every run; the rule sits behind `ITalentPointSource`, so it can be swapped later. Respec is free in the Hub: refund one rank at a time if nothing depends on it, or press R twice to refund everything.
- **Saving:** one `talents` field at the end of `SaveData`. Old saves load with no talents. A save with unknown or invalid talents is repaired on load, and refunded entirely if it is still invalid or spends more than was earned.
- **The screen:** T in the Hub, with keyboard and mouse, tooltips with exact numbers, and locked / available / owned / maxed states. A small "T 天赋 · 可用 N 点" label sits in the Hub's top-right corner. The bot spends its points automatically, and `[TALENT]` log lines are written.
