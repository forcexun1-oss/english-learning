📝 Natural English: "Is it just about asking him to produce a new one for us?"
💡 Tip: Chinese yes/no questions ending in 吗 need an inverted auxiliary in English (Is it...? / Are we...?), not just statement word order. Here 转…出来 is best rendered as 'produce' or 'generate' rather than the literal 'turn out'.
