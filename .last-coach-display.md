📝 Natural English: "We need to commit? What do you mean—doesn't it save to disk?"
💡 Tip: “怎么个意思” is colloquial for “what does that mean?” — in English, you can say “What do you mean?” or “What's that about?” Also, “存盘” often simply means “save,” so “save to disk” works but sounds a bit literal.
