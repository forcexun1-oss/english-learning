📝 Natural English: "What do you mean? So it wasn't done right?"
💡 Tip: "没搞好" is casual for "not done properly/successfully." In a coding context, "wasn't done right" or "didn't work" are natural equivalents — "搞" is an informal verb meaning "do/get done," often better rendered as "get it working" rather than a literal "fix."
