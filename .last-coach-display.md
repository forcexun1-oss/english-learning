📝 Natural English: "It's too small; I can't see anything."
💡 Tip: The phrase '看个锤子' is a colloquial Chinese slang expressing frustration or negation (similar to 'see nothing' or 'for nothing'). In professional English, simply stating 'I can't see anything' or 'It's too small to see' is clearer and more polite.
