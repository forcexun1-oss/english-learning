📝 Natural English: "What should I reply?"
💡 Tip: In English, indirect questions keep statement word order: "What do you want me to reply?" — not "What do you want me to reply what?" Also, "reply to X" needs the preposition when an object follows ("reply to this message"), but not when it stands alone.
