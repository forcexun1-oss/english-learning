- **Minions:** the Die state is there and does fire. Every rig has the 0.8 s `Death_A` clip, and the probe logged `die=True` from about 0.3 s after the kill. But the first frames are hidden under the existing white flash and bone-spark burst. Then the body vanished in a single frame at 1.4 s (`Destroy(gameObject, 1.4f)`, no fade). So it read as "no death". The screenshots show the skeleton falling backwards and lying there, then simply gone.
- **Boss:** `RunDirector` subscribed `BossDefeated += () =&gt; EndRun(true)`, so the result screen opened in the same frame. The probe logged `phase=Result` at +0.00 s. The Die clip played behind the results UI.
- **Player:** `Die()` was already triggered, but the result screen opened in the same frame, so the collapse was never visible.

## What changed
- **Separate bug found and fixed (commit 880d7d0):** after a lost boss fight, the boss was never removed. It is not parented to the arena, so the room reset missed it, and it kept fighting in the next run. In my first bot run it killed the player in room 1 after 19 s. `ResetRoom` now destroys it. This is on main today, independent of the death work.
- **`Actors/Corpse.cs`** (new): every dying actor plays its Die clip, or tips over procedurally if the model has none. It lies still for 1.2 s, puffs dust when it lands, then sinks and shrinks over 0.6 s and is destroyed. The HP bar hides, and dead actors can't be hit, targeted or collided with.
  - `Enemy.Collapse()` kills a minion without counting it as a kill or dropping loot. The boss death uses it.
  - The boss now stops its in-progress move when it dies.
- **`Features/BossDeath/BossDeathModule.cs`** (new module, 2.5 s, hooked to `BossDefeated` and `PhaseChanged`):
  - hit-stop on the killing blow, then 0.4x slow motion for 1.5 s easing back over 0.3 s;
  - camera push-in via `PushShot`/`ClearShot`, plus letterbox bars;
  - dust, ember and bone burst with a shake when the body lands (about 1.63 s);
  - remaining minions, projectiles and ground warnings are cleared.
- **Slow motion without writing `Time.timeScale`:** it temporarily lowers `Juice.BaseScale` and goes through `HitStop`/`ResetTime`, so `Juice.cs` is untouched.
- **`RunDirector`:** waits 2.5 s after the boss dies and 1.6 s after the player dies before showing results. It counts in real time × normal game speed, so the 4x bot still finishes, and the countdown stops while paused.
- **`Core/DeathTimeline.cs`:** the pure timing logic, covered by `Tests/Editor/DeathTimelineTests.cs`. One line added to `docs/architecture/overview.md`.
