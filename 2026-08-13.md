=== DESIGN CONSTRAINT (the important one) ===

The sidecar changes ONLY where bytes come from, never how they are interpreted.
Build every array with the EXISTING handle: `hlist[expert_id]._array_from_buffer(slice)`.
Do not re-derive dtype or shape from the index -- the index is used only to
locate and split bytes. This keeps one authority for interpretation and means a
bad index cannot silently reinterpret good bytes.
