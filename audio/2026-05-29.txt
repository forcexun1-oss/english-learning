# English Learning — 2026-05-29

Today's log covered Rockchip board testing, YOLO26 vision APIs, QR code detection, LLM context limits, and UI tweaks. Language for benchmarking and integration work.

## Vocabulary to Learn

.  benchmark – standardized performance test. "Run the bench to verify the new build."
.  class names map – labels for detected object IDs. "Add class names to VisionConfig."
.  localization – finding where something is in an image. YOLO is strong here; QR decoding is a separate step.
.  context length (-c) – how many tokens the LLM can remember. "What is the max value of -c?"
.  integrate – add a library or feature into the project. "Integrate WeChatCV instead."
.  thumbnail – small preview image in a list. "Keep thumbnails in the list view."

## Grammar and Natural Phrasing


"What's the progress so far?" is a natural way to check on long-running agent tasks. Be specific when two devices are connected: "Push to the phone — I have two devices plugged in."

## Handy Questions

.  "Show me the YOLO API's input parameters."
.  "Can YOLO26 recognize QR codes?"
