## Method
- **Ear body:** the ear is not cut. I Taubin-smooth the ear vertices in place, so the topology and watertightness are unchanged and no fragment is left. The weight ramps to zero near protected triangles, and the ear's normals are recomputed.
- **Fringe:** the rim is extracted from the smoothed ear by angular bins in a PCA ear plane. Cards then follow the rim tangent with roots sunk into the rim, using the white atlas tufts.
