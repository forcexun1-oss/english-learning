## Your Most Common Mistakes

1. **Verb form in questions** — Use *Did X cause…?* not *Does X caused…?*; use *has* with *it*, not *have*.
2. **Word choice** — *from scratch* (not *by scratch*); *every day* (each day) vs *everyday* (adjective).
3. **Uncountable nouns** — *code* and *progress*, not *codes* / *progeress*.
4. **Prepositions with Git** — *push to main*, *pull from the repo* (not *push on*).
5. **After auxiliaries** — *didn't log* (base verb), not *didn't logged*.

These showed up while you talked to **Claude Code and Cursor** about automation, MCP, and repo sync — very normal for fast debugging speech.
