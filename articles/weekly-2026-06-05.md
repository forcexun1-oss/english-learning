## Your Most Common Mistakes

1. **Third-person singular** — *It doesn't* (not *It don't*); *Does it include…?* (not *Does it caused…?*).
2. **Auxiliaries + base verb** — *Did you write them into one plan?* (not mixed tense); *has rebooted* → then *verify* for commands.
3. **Prepositions & idioms** — *from scratch* (not *by scratch*); *according to the OTA design* (not *if following*); *on port 8080* / *port 8080* (name the port before the number).
4. **Spelling & capitalization** — *occurred*, *deploy*, *Switch* (not *Swich*); capitalize **Rockchip**, **TTS**, **LLM**, and sentence starts; use **board** (not *plate*) for dev hardware.
5. **Agent-command clarity** — Prefer *Summarize today's errors* over *summarize error occured today*; *Verify that the service has rebooted* beats bare *verify* after a reboot note.

Most slips appeared under time pressure while steering agents through logs, ports, and failed background tasks—not in casual chat.

## Vocabulary & Tips Recap

- **Repo & automation:** *commit and push*, *pull before writing files*, *scheduled task*, *Do not open a pull request*, *exit without changes*.
- **Embedded / edge:** *development board*, *compile and deploy*, *get the pipeline working* (跑通), *fully implemented*, *must* vs *should* in specs.
- **API debugging:** *test via HTTP*, *failed with exit code*, *Test passed* = correct result, not just HTTP 200; *Explain the API call flow*.
- **Structure & Git:** *push to main*, *keep branches consistent*, *store models under `/var/lib`*; *Both X and Y* for paired moves.
- **Natural listing:** *things like …*, Oxford comma in lists; *from a design perspective* for 从设计上讲.
