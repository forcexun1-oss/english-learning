## Your Most Common Mistakes

1. **Subject–verb agreement** — *This code doesn't work* (not *dont work*); *Codex can use it too, right?* (not *codex也能用是把*).
2. **Informal Chinese phrasing left untranslated** — *干得咋样* → *How is it going?* / *What's the progress?*; *看看他咋样了* → *Check on its status*; *阻塞* → *blocker* or *blocking issue*.
3. **Question form & capitalization** — Start new sentences with a capital letter (*Is it running on NPU/CPU?*); use inverted word order (*What is our ASR target?* not *我们的ASR目标到底是啥*).
4. **Sequence & approval words** — *Then write the plan* (not *That write*); *That works* / *Sounds good* for 可以; *show me for review* for 给我过目.
5. **Technical register** — Prefer *investigate* / *research* over casual 调研; *commit process* or *how commits are handled* for 提交法; *performance metrics* for 性能数据; *currently deployed* for 现在部署的.

Most slips appeared while multitasking—parallel agents, board storage limits, and long pasted agent reports—not in simple one-line commands.
