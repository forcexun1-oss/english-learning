## Your Most Common Mistakes

1. **Spelling under pressure** — *generate* (not *genrate*), *summary* (not *summery*), *compile* (not *complie*), *service* (not *servcie*), *conversion* (not *convertion*), *consumed* (not *comsumed*), *organized* (not *agonized*).
2. **Typos in agent commands** — *following* env variables (not *flowing*); *without* the coach (not *whihout*); *Option 3* (not *Opton 3*); *I've rebooted* (not *I'v reboot it*).
3. **Question word order** — *What is deployed for ASR?* (not *what deployed is*); invert subject and verb in questions (*Is the port open?*).
4. **Verb forms after modals / imperatives** — *start cross-compiling* (gerund after *start*); *Did you write* (not *Is written by you*); use *decode* / *decoding* (verb), not *decoder* (noun).
