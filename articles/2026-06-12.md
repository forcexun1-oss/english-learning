## Vocabulary

- **prefill cache** – reuses already-computed prompt tokens on the NPU; useful when benchmarking repeated sessions in `llm-bench`.
- **cross-compile** – build a binary on your Mac for the ARM board, e.g. `middleware-service` for the phone.
- **cold start / physical reboot** – power-cycle the board when a service dies after one inference run.
- **proxy port** – the local port (8765) that forwards traffic to the LLM backend.

## Grammar for Agent Instructions

| Rough | Better |
|-------|--------|
| *Did llm proxy service crashed?* | Did the **LLM proxy service crash**? (use base verb after *did*) |
| *is the port 8765 opened* | **Is port 8765 open?** |
| *Are the logs cleared when the servcie restart?* | Are the logs cleared when the **service restarts**? |
| *Handle the middleware servcie* | Handle the **middleware service** (watch spelling) |
| *Opton 3* | **Option 3** |

When a service stops after you unplug the phone, state the hypothesis clearly: *Was it killed when I disconnected the phone? That must be the reason—fix it.* For hook issues, separate cause from symptom: *I installed OMX again, then all hooks are registered twice* is stronger than *the hooks are a mess*. Prefer bounded requests: *Do not clear logs—just add a max limit.*
