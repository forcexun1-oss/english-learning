## Vocabulary

- **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition)** – speech-to-text; here deployed as a server on port 8081.
- **encoder / decoder** – model parts; you asked whether `Qwen3-ASR-0.6B` ships only an encoder and must pair with an omni model for decoding.
- **deployment plan** – how a model is installed, started, and verified on the board.
- **handoff** – whether another engineer can take over with clear docs and steps.
- **model loading error** – RKNN init failure, often tied to wrong tensor shape or layout (e.g. `[1, 1, 128, 100]` vs `[8, 1, 128, 100]`).
