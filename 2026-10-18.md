## What was built
All code is in `Features/Pickups/` under `unity/Assets/Scripts/Game/`. `PickupsModule` is a `[GameModule]` that is discovered automatically; I did not edit `GameApp.cs`, `GameEvents.cs`, or anything in `Actors`, `Bosses`, `World`, `Flow` or `Ui`.
- **Files:** `PickupsModule.cs` (scene side), `PickupRules.cs` (pure logic), `PickupEvents.cs`, `PickupText.cs` (the floating "+N", copied from the `DamageNumber` approach).
- **Docs and tests:** `docs/specs/pickups.md`, plus `PickupDropPolicyTests.cs` and `PickupTimingTests.cs` in `Assets/Tests/Editor/`.
- **Bus events:** `PickupSpawned`, `PickupCollected`, `PickupExpired`, and `ShardsChanged(Total)`, which is published with 0 at run start.
- **Cleanup:** pickups are parented under `Arena.Root`, so rooms clean them up. They are also cleared on Hub/Result and reset when a new run starts.

## Tuning numbers
- **Health orb:**
  - drops on 18% of minion kills, or 34% when the player is below 40% HP;
  - a pity timer guarantees one on the 7th kill in a row without a drop;
  - heals 12% of max HP.
- **Shard coin:** 8% chance when no orb dropped, worth +1 each.
- **Full HP rule:** an orb is not collected and ignores the magnet at full HP; it waits on the floor until the player needs it.
- **Placement:** it lands 0.8–1.8 m from the dead minion, clamped to the arena and outside `Arena.Obstacles`, after a 0.5 s arc.
- **Lifetime:** 14 s, blinking in the last 3 s (faster in the last 1.5 s). The timer only runs while `ctx.Running`, so pausing stops it.
- **Magnet and collect:** pull starts within 3.5 m and speeds up from 3 to 16 m/s on a quadratic ramp. Collect happens at 0.8 m.
- **Collect effect:** sparkles, a ring and a floating "+N". The "+N" is hidden when the `damageNumbers` setting is off.

## Tests
- **Result:** 224 passed, 0 failed, including my 10 new tests. The 231 you quoted is presumably from a newer main; this branch's baseline without mine is 214.
- **Coverage:** seeded determinism, pity timer, drop rates and the low-HP bonus (statistical bounds over 4000 rolls), blink and expiry, the magnet curve, the full-HP rule, and placement avoiding obstacles and the arena edge.
