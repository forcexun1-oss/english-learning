## 1. make_resident at init — lazy or eager?

`make_resident` is NOT called at init for all entries. The no-copy init pass (Change G) does two things at startup:

1. Calls `ggml_moe_gpu_slab_buffer_create_nocopy(...)` → creates 120 Metal buffer objects (40 layers × 3 projections), each wrapping the full tensor's page-aligned virtual address range. **No physical page faults.** The OS does not fault mmap pages on `newBufferWithBytesNoCopy` — they remain file-backed and cold.

2. Writes metadata into `entry.gpu_slab_slices[pi]` (`slot`, `buffer_index`, `slot_stride`, `slot_data_off`). No `publish_resident` called here.

`publish_resident` (which populates `gpu_slab_by_key`) is called only from `gpu_slab_stage_entries` → triggered by `touch_many` → triggered by `cb_eval` firing on each layer's topk tensor at decode time. Only the selected top-k experts per layer are staged per token.

**Physical pages accessed per token: top-k × 40 layers × 3 projections × 1,114,112 bytes.** For k=8: 8 × 40 × 3 × 1.07 MiB ≈ **1.02 GiB** hot pages per forward pass.

**Mapped-resident (virtual) per layer:** 3 × 256 × 1,114,112 = **815.6 MiB/layer × 40 = 31.88 GiB total virtual.**
